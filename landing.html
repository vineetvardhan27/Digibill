WHAT DIGIBILL DOES

Built for how Indian shops actually work

AI Bill Scanning

Photograph any invoice. Digibill reads the supplier name, amount, and due date in seconds using Groq Vision AI.

Supplier Portal

Give suppliers their own login. They view invoices, acknowledge bills, raise disputes, and upload their version — without calling you.

Payment Reminders

Bills due tomorrow shouldn't surprise you. Automated WhatsApp and email reminders go out 3 days before, so nothing slips.

Health Scores

Every supplier gets a credit score based on your payment history. Know who's reliable before you overextend.

Cash Flow Forecast

See money going out over the next 30 and 90 days, based on your bill history. Plan inventory purchases around what's coming.

GST Line Items

Log CGST, SGST, and IGST per line item. Export clean GST summaries when filing time comes.

How it works

Add your suppliers

Log or scan bills

Track, remind, resolve

Dashboard shows what's pending, what's overdue, and what disputes need your attention.
